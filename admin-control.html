Academic Setup Policy & Guidance

  • Backend Authority: Student options are served dynamically from PostgreSQL tables without hardcoded frontend dropdowns.
  • Dependent Selection: Students choose University first -> Courses under University -> Batches under Course.
  • No Destructive Deletes: Universities, Courses, or Batches with active student references cannot be hard-deleted. Use ARCHIVED or INACTIVE.
  • Admin Reassignment: Profile reassignments require a valid audit reason and update student access on their next request.
