About College OS

Built for students who want more than just academics.

College OS is a student-first platform designed to make college life more organized, productive and growth-driven.

Instead of keeping study materials, updates, opportunities and support scattered across different places, College OS brings them into one clean, connected and professional system.

It helps students learn better, contribute value, earn recognition, join a meaningful community and stay connected with internships, hackathons and career opportunities.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How will students join the community?

After login, students will receive an email from College OS with official community joining details.

Can students upload notes?

Yes. Students can upload useful notes and resources. Approved uploads may help them earn points and recognition.

How will students earn points?

Students may earn points through uploads, referrals, quizzes and platform activity.

What kind of rewards can students get?

Eligible students may receive certificates, recognition, goodies and possible voucher opportunities.

Is admin login visible publicly?

No. Admin login is hidden and not shown on the public landing page.
