Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This policy explains the information College OS uses to provide student learning, community, support, and account services.

Information we use

Depending on the features you use, this may include your name, email address, mobile number, academic selections, uploaded content, activity and progress data, support messages, and account security records.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate accounts, provide personalized learning features, process uploads, operate community and support features, prevent abuse, improve reliability, and communicate service-related information.

Cookies and sessions

College OS uses essential cookies for secure sessions, authentication, CSRF protection, preferences, and service operation. These cookies may be HTTP-only and are not used to store passwords or readable session tokens in page content.

Sharing and storage

We use service providers needed to operate the platform, such as hosting, email delivery, database, and storage providers. Access is limited to the purpose of providing or protecting the service. Uploaded files follow the visibility and authorization rules of the feature that stores them.

Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of account data by emailing support@collegeo.in from the account email. We may verify your identity and retain limited records where required for security, legal, or operational reasons.

Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests can be sent to support@collegeo.in.
